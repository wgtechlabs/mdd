## Direction contract

THESIS: A field guide to the user's project. Content is immediately readable, with persistent chapter and section context.

OWN-WORLD: White reading paper, pale cool-gray navigation ground, deep slate text, forest-teal active links. One system sans for reading and navigation; monospace only for source examples. Flat surfaces and fine rules.

STORY: Find a chapter, understand its instructions, copy a useful example, and continue to the next topic. Markdown access sits beside reading tools.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 64px masthead above a 248px chapter rail, a generous 70ch article, and a compact right-hand outline. The article title belongs to its source Markdown; no duplicate hero. On phones, chapters become a native disclosure above the article and the local outline is another disclosure.

Light follows daytime reading; system dark mode supports low-light reading. Motion is short control feedback with reduced-motion support, never entrance choreography.
